Where quantum meets the real world.

Turning quantum potential into practical value across key industries.

Quantum computing promises to solve problems that are too large or too complex for even the most powerful classical supercomputers — by harnessing the laws of nature itself.

At ORIENTOM, we focus on the industries where that promise matters most: finance, energy, materials, mobility and bio-health. Working closely with industrial and academic partners, we transform quantum potential into practical algorithms that create measurable value.

Q-Finance, Smarter decisions at market speed.

Financial markets run on complex calculations —
pricing, risk management and portfolio decisions that must be fast and accurate.
Quantum amplitude estimation promises a quadratic
speed-up over classical Monte Carlo simulation,

while quantum optimization opens new ways to build portfolios.
Our vision is to bring quantum advantage into everyday financial decision-making.

Q-Electric Grid, Powering a smarter, greener grid.

Quantum optimization for reliable, renewable-ready power networks.

As renewable energy and electric vehicles reshape power systems, grids must balance supply and demand in real time across countless variables.
Quantum optimization can tackle hard problems such as unit commitment, power-flow analysis and resilience planning.
We envision smarter, cleaner and more reliable energy networks.

Q-Material, Designing the materials of tomorrow.

Simulating molecules in nature's own language to discover new materials.

Discovering new materials for batteries, catalysts and semiconductors requires understanding molecules at the quantum level — something classical computers can only approximate. Quantum computers simulate nature in its own language, enabling more accurate electronic-structure calculations. Our goal is to accelerate the discovery of next-generation materials.

Q-Traffic, Moving cities forward.

Quantum optimization and quantum AI for efficient, low-emission mobility.

Urban mobility is a massive optimization challenge — routing vehicles, coordinating traffic signals and scheduling logistics under constantly changing conditions. Quantum optimization and quantum machine learning offer new ways to reduce congestion and emissions.
We work toward faster, cleaner and more efficient transportation networks.

Q-Biohealth,
Accelerating discovery
for human health.

Quantum-enhanced
simulation and AI for drug

Drug discovery, protein design and
genomics involve astronomically
large search spaces.
Quantum computing can enhance
molecular simulation,

AI-driven design of new drugs and antibodies.

Our goal is to make the path from
discovery to patient shorter,
cheaper and more precise.
