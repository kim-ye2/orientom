Atom-Q , One interface. Every quantum computer.

Atom-Q™ is ORIENTOM's quantum middleware — the bridge between your applications and quantum hardware.
It compiles, optimizes and routes quantum circuits to superconducting, trapped-ion, neutral-atom and photonic processors,
as well as to high-performance simulators, through a single unified interface. Developers write their algorithms once and deploy them anywhere.
Enterprises stay free from vendor lock-in and can always choose the best hardware for each workload as the quantum landscape evolves.

Unified access to multiple quantum
hardware backends and simulators
Hardware-aware circuit compilation
and optimization
Hybrid quantum–classical workflow
orchestration with HPC / GPU resources
Flexible enterprise deployment
— on-premise or cloud

Titanium-Q, Run quantum algorithms today — no quantum computer required.

Titanium-Q is a high-performance quantum software emulator that reproduces the behavior of quantum processors on classical computers.
It enables researchers and enterprises to develop, debug and benchmark quantum algorithms at scale
— cost-effectively and without waiting in hardware queues.
Combined with Atom-Q™, Titanium-Q provides a smooth path from emulation to real quantum hardware:
validate your results first, then move to quantum devices with confidence.

High-performance simulation on CPU / GPU-accelerated infrastructure
Noise modeling that mimics the behavior of real quantum hardware
Seamless integration with Atom-Q™ for emulation-to-hardware workflows
Ideal for algorithm R&D, proof-of-concept (PoC) projects and education

Finance Applications  , Quantum algorithms for faster, deeper financial insight.

Pricing, risk and portfolio decisions rely on massive computations — often millions of Monte Carlo scenarios.
ORIENTOM develops quantum and quantum-inspired algorithms that target exactly these bottlenecks.
Together with financial institutions and academic partners, we are building ready-to-use engines for derivatives pricing,
portfolio optimization and risk analytics — designed to run on today's emulators and tomorrow's quantum computers.

Derivatives pricing with Quantum Amplitude Estimation (QAE) — Asian, barrier and multi-asset options
Portfolio optimization with QAOA and hybrid quantum–classical solvers
Risk analytics, including VaR and CVaR estimation
Quantum physics-informed neural networks (q-PINN) for PDE-based pricing models
